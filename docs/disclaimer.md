# The disclaimer

Printed on every quotation, tested, and never removable. **This is the draft.** The final wording is
settled in Block 3, where `QuoteDocument` is built and a test asserts the text is present under every
combination of settings.

It is written here in Block 0 so that no screen is ever built without it in view, and so the wording
has time to be read by someone who builds before it is ever sent to a client.

---

> Quantities are estimates for budgeting, not structural design. Concrete class, bar sizes, spacing
> and splice lengths come from the plans or a licensed engineer. A quotation is not an official
> receipt or invoice. Prices are valid only until the date shown.

---

## Why each sentence is there

| Sentence | What it protects against |
|---|---|
| Estimates for budgeting, not structural design | Tantya counts materials for a design someone else is responsible for. It has no idea whether the wall will stand up |
| Class, bar sizes, spacing and splices come from the plans or an engineer | Block 5 asks for bar spacing. A number typed into a field must never read as a recommendation |
| Not an official receipt or invoice | A BIR-registered receipt is a different document with different legal weight |
| Prices valid only until the date shown | Hardware prices move, and a quote is a commitment. Block 3's "valid until" is what bounds it |
